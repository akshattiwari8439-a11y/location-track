About Us

Indrawar Sweets Place has proudly served families in Bidhuna for decades, preserving the essence of Indian mithai craftsmanship. Our sweets are prepared with traditional recipes, hygienic methods, and premium ingredients.

With years of experience and a commitment to purity, we ensure every sweet and snack carries the same homemade warmth and trusted quality.

Experience: Trusted by the local community for quality sweets and festive catering.
